Why isn't it on the Mac App Store?

App Store apps must run in Apple's sandbox, and a sandboxed app can't quit other apps, which is RamClean's whole job. So RamClean is sold directly, signed with an Apple Developer ID and notarized by Apple, and updates itself securely.
