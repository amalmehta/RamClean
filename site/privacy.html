In short: nothing leaves your Mac except the licence check and the update check. RamClean has no accounts, no analytics, no crash reporting and no advertising.

What stays on your Mac

Everything RamClean learns and measures is stored only on your Mac, in ~/Library/Application Support/RamClean/ and RamClean's preferences:

  • which apps you use and for how long, and when they launch and quit
  • your Quit and Keep answers, and the model trained from them
  • disk scan results: folder names and sizes
  • feedback you write in the app

None of this is sent anywhere. Deleting that folder removes it.

What leaves your Mac

Only two things, and only to the places named here:

1. The licence check

  • When: you activate a licence key, about once a day while one is active, and when you deactivate it.
  • Sent to: Lemon Squeezy (api.lemonsqueezy.com), our reseller.
  • What: the licence key, an activation ID, and your Mac's name (as set in System Settings → General → Sharing), so you can tell your Macs apart in your list of activations. Like any web request, it also carries your IP address.

2. The update check

  • When: about once a day if you allow automatic checks, or when you choose Check for Updates.
  • Sent to: our update feed, REPLACE-ME.example.com.
  • What: a request for the list of versions, which names RamClean's version in its header, and your IP address. No system profile is sent.

During the free trial, with no key entered, RamClean makes no licence requests at all.

Buying

Purchases are handled by Lemon Squeezy as merchant of record. They collect your name, email, payment and billing details to process the order, calculate tax and send your licence key. We receive your name, email and order details from them, to provide support and refunds. See Lemon Squeezy's privacy policy.

This website

This site is plain pages with no cookies, analytics or trackers. The host keeps standard server logs (IP address, page, time) for security.

Your choices

  • Turn off automatic update checks in RamClean → Settings → General.
  • Deactivate your licence in Settings → Licence, which deletes the activation from Lemon Squeezy's list.
